# Source verification

This note records the external sources used to close the final manuscript sourcing checks. These checks change citation support and descriptive wording only; they do not change any OpenDrift/OpenOil result.

## Jubilee crude property basis

Primary reference for the manuscript:

**Tullow Oil plc (2019), Jubilee Crude Assay 2019, assay JUBILEE2019.**

Values used in the manuscript:
- API gravity: 37.41°
- Density at 15 °C: 0.8373 kg/L = 837.3 kg m⁻³
- Kinematic viscosity at 40 °C: 4.6 cSt
- Pour point: 3 °C

The Appenteng et al. (2013) characterization is retained as an independent historical measurement, not as the primary property source.

## Jubilee stochastic OILMAP precedent

Direct primary source:

**Environmental Resources Management and Tullow Ghana Limited (2009), Jubilee Field Phase 1 Development Environmental Impact Statement, Chapter 5.**

The EIS documents ASA OILMAP stochastic modelling with 500 independent simulations using different start times for each spill scenario. The manuscript cites the 2009 EIS for this modelling precedent.

## Carvalho et al. (2025)

**Carvalho, I. V., Mendonça, L. F. F., and Fernandes, E. H. L. (2025), Marine Pollution Bulletin 221, 118527.**

This paper is used only for the narrow claim that API-gravity changes had limited influence on advective displacement in the tested MEDSLIK-II case while materially affecting evaporation. It is not used as support for the viscosity–emulsification–Ohnesorge mechanism reconstructed in the present OpenOil analysis.
